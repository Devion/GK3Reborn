# Save schema 4

Conversation counts now use `ACTOR|NOUN|TOPIC` keys. Individual spoken topic lines
also include the actor, followed by the existing U+0001-separated fields. Gabriel
and Grace no longer inherit each other's introductions or exhausted topics.

Schemas 1–3 still load. Their unscoped conversation records cannot identify who
spoke. Day-one and day-two 7 AM records are assigned to Gabriel, which makes
Grace's morning introductions available. Completed morning introductions are
recovered from their individual score events. Later records are assigned to the saved
protagonist. Ambiguous conversations may consequently be offered again; score
events remain unchanged and cannot award duplicate points. New saves preserve
each protagonist's history independently.
